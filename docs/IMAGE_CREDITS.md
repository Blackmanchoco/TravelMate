## Boundaries

Thirteen photographs are applied to four Destinations and nine Places, only in the demo database and seed source. No main database or schema change is included. Other objects retain placeholders. KLCC Park is a historical photograph from 2006, not evidence of current visitor facilities. Batu Caves uses an interior view. Merdeka Square awaits explicit subject confirmation; Thean Hou Temple awaits an acceptable crop or replacement. Hero remains conditional and unchanged. Login and Register use blue theme panels; the rejected Login photo and unapproved Register photo are not used. Church of Our Lady of Lourdes remains on hold because it has no existing Place.
